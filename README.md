# StoreSalesAnalysis
Store Sales Analysis Dashboard

An Excel dashboard analyzing 34,500 orders (2023–2025) to find out where a store makes and loses money.

Dashboard
Key numbers
Sales: $5.87M
Profit: $970K (16.5% margin)
Orders: 34,500
Return rate: 5.5%
What I found
Electronics brings in the most profit ($344K), but at the lowest margin of the profitable categories (10.4%).
Grocery loses money (-$9K) even though it has over 4,000 orders.
Returns are highest in the East (5.9%) and lowest in Central (5.1%).
Region, payment method, age and gender barely affect margin. Category is what matters.
What's inside
Dashboard: KPI cards, charts, and slicers to filter by gender, age group and date
Profit By Category: PivotTables and charts
Sales: the full dataset
